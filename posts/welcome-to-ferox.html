Rebuilding ferox.eti.br with HydePHP

Why I moved this site to HydePHP, what I want from a personal website, and why static output still makes sense to me.

Aug 13th, 2026 By Ferox Filed under Notes

I rebuilt this site because I wanted my personal website to feel closer to how I actually work.

That means a few things in practice:

  • Content should live in plain files.
  • Pages should compile to static HTML.
  • Design should feel deliberate without depending on a fragile frontend stack.

HydePHP is a good fit for that approach. It gives me Markdown for writing, Blade for custom presentation, and a build process that stays understandable from end to end.

What you can expect here

Most posts here will fall into a few buckets:

  • PHP, Laravel, and Drupal work
  • automation and scripting notes
  • experiments with static publishing
  • opinions about FLOSS and long-lived websites

Why a personal site still matters

Publishing on your own domain is still the cleanest way to keep control of your writing. Feeds, search, archives, and permalinks all work better when the content has a stable home.

That is the standard this site is aiming for.
