# Fernando “ferox” dos Santos

I am a Full Stack developer focused on **PHP**, **Laravel**, **Drupal**, automation, and script writing.

This site is my own corner of the web, where I can publish with fewer constraints and keep the content close to the tools I actually use.

## What I work with

- PHP as a practical foundation for shipping real projects
- Laravel for application work and modern PHP workflows
- Drupal for content-heavy and structured web projects
- automation and scripting to reduce repetitive operational work
- FLOSS technologies whenever the decision is mine to make

## How I think about software

I like software that is:

- readable
- maintainable
- inspectable
- portable
- useful to the people who depend on it

That usually pushes me toward simple deployments, clear code, and fewer unnecessary layers.

## Outside the code

I care about open source culture, community, and the politics of the web. I have described myself publicly as a **Fedora Ambassador** and a **Pirate**, and both say something about how I approach technology: openness matters.

Life is a continuous learning process. Work makes me more rigorous. Travel makes life more colorful.
